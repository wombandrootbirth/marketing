## This week's intention

Plant seeds with the people who hold mothers every day, so the right families find their way to you.

## Reach out to (4)

**1. Hospital childbirth-education and lactation teams**
Mothers recovering from a cesarean birth are exactly who your in-hospital postpartum care serves.
- Where to start *(look up & verify the right contact)*: MultiCare Tacoma General (Tacoma),
  St. Joseph Medical Center (Tacoma), MultiCare Good Samaritan (Puyallup),
  St. Francis Hospital (Federal Way), MultiCare Auburn Medical Center, Valley Medical Center (Renton)
- Who to ask for: the childbirth-education coordinator or the lactation services lead
- How: email, then a friendly follow-up call a week later

> Hello [Name], my name is Darlene Johnson, founder of Womb & Root Birthing Services here in
> South King and Pierce County. We offer postpartum support at home and in the hospital,
> including gentle care for mothers healing after a cesarean birth. I would love to share our
> information with your families and learn how we might support your team. Would you be open to a
> short conversation, or may I drop off a few cards? With warmth, Darlene

**2. Midwifery practices and birth centers**
Their families already seek a holistic birth, so they're a natural fit for you.
- *(look up & verify)* midwives and birth centers in Tacoma, Puyallup, Federal Way, Kent and Auburn
- Who to ask for: the practice owner or office manager

> Hello [Name], I so admire the care your practice gives families. I'm Darlene, founder of
> Womb & Root Birthing Services. We offer birth and postpartum doula support, placenta
> encapsulation, belly binding, and womb wellness care. I'd be honored to be a trusted resource
> for your clients. Could I bring by a small welcome basket and our referral cards?

**3. Prenatal chiropractors and pelvic floor therapists**
They see pregnant clients weekly and are often asked, "Do you know a good doula?"
- *(look up & verify)* chiropractors listing "Webster technique" or "prenatal" near your area

**4. Military family support at Joint Base Lewis-McChord**
Military spouses often give birth far from family and need postpartum support.
- *(look up & verify)* JBLM new-parent support programs and military spouse groups
- Ask whether you can share a flyer or offer a free talk, such as "Preparing for Your Fourth Trimester"
