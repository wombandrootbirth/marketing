# Outreach Email — Chiropractors

*Darlene's own words. Use for prenatal and postpartum chiropractors (rotation week 4 in
`marketing-plan/networking-directory.md`).*

---

## Subject line ideas

- A postpartum care partnership for your patients
- Supporting your postpartum patients between adjustments
- Referral partnership: Womb & Root postpartum doula care

---

## Email

Hi [Name],

I'm Darlene Johnson, founder of Womb & Root Birthing Services, a postpartum doula agency serving families across Pierce and South King County. I'm reaching out because our work overlaps in a meaningful way — many of the families you're treating for postpartum alignment and pelvic recovery are also navigating sleepless nights, newborn care, and recovery on their own at home.

Womb & Root offers in-home postpartum support — overnight care, day support, and recovery-focused add-ons like postpartum yoni steaming — so your patients have more consistent support between adjustments, and more capacity to actually rest and heal.

I'd love to explore a referral partnership: we'd be glad to share your practice with our clients as a trusted resource for postpartum body work, and would welcome the same consideration when your patients could use hands-on care at home. Would you be open to a short call or a coffee sometime in the next few weeks? I'm also happy to drop off information for your front desk.

Warmly,
Darlene Johnson
Founder, Womb & Root Birthing Services
(206) 409-9747 · info@wombandrootbirth.com · wombandrootbirth.com
