# Thank-You & Partnership Email — Midwifery Birth Center at St. Joseph

*Sent after Darlene interviewed for a doula position there (October 2026). She wasn't selected,
and is following up with gratitude and a request to share postpartum resources. A good model
for any "thank you, and may we partner?" note.*

**Subject:** Thank you, and a postpartum resource for your families

Dear [Nurse Manager's Name],

Thank you so much for taking the time to meet with me about the doula position at the Midwifery Birth Center at St. Joseph. Although I wasn't selected this time, I truly enjoyed learning about your team and the beautiful, family-centered care you offer, and I'm grateful for the opportunity to connect.

As the founder of Womb & Root Birthing Services, I provide in-home postpartum doula care across Pierce and South King County, including overnight and day support, lactation support, and gentle care in the hospital after a cesarean birth. We also accept Apple Health. Would you be open to my leaving brochures at the birth center, or sharing our information as a resource for families heading home? I'd be honored to support your families in the weeks after birth.

Warmly,
