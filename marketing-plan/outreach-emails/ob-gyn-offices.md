# Outreach Email — OB-GYN Offices

*Darlene's own words. Use for smaller, independent OB-GYN and women's health offices (rotation
week 8 in `marketing-plan/networking-directory.md`).*

---

> **Use with smaller, independent offices only.** Large systems such as Healthpoint, MultiCare,
> and Franciscan usually have policies against outside doula brochures or referrals. If you're
> unsure, call the front desk first and ask whether they share community resources with patients.

## Subject line ideas

- Postpartum support resource for your patients
- In-home postpartum care for your patients (Apple Health accepted)
- Introducing Womb & Root Birthing Services

---

## Email

Hello,

My name is Darlene Johnson, and I'm the founder of Womb & Root Birthing Services, a postpartum doula agency providing in-home care for families across Pierce and South King County. I wanted to introduce our agency, since many of your patients leave their postpartum visits without much support in place for the weeks ahead.

Womb & Root offers overnight support, day support, postpartum meal prep, and holistic recovery care like postpartum yoni steaming — all in-home, and available through Medicaid/Apple Health as well as private pay. Our goal is to give new parents real, hands-on help during recovery, not just information.

Would your office be open to keeping a few of our brochures on hand for patients at postpartum visits, or including us as a resource in discharge materials? I'd be glad to stop by and introduce myself, or send information for your team to review first — whichever is easier.

Thank you for the care you provide to families in our community.

Warmly,
Darlene Johnson
Founder, Womb & Root Birthing Services
(206) 409-9747 · info@wombandrootbirth.com · wombandrootbirth.com

---

## Before sending

- **Have brochures ready.** This email offers them, so bring or attach one.
- **Confirm your Apple Health details.** Medical offices may ask exactly which services Apple
  Health covers and how you bill, so it helps to have a short answer ready.
- **Ask for the right person.** Big clinics often have an office manager or a patient-education
  lead who decides what goes in the waiting room.
