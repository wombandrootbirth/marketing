## Subject line ideas

## Email

Hi [Name],

My name is Darlene Johnson, and I'm the founder of Womb & Root Birthing Services, a postpartum doula agency serving Pierce and South King County. I know how much thought you put into preparing your clients for birth — and how quickly that same family can feel unsupported once you've stepped back and the fourth trimester begins.

Womb & Root fills that gap with in-home postpartum care: overnight support, day support, meal prep, and holistic offerings like postpartum yoni steaming, all built to help families actually rest and recover. We also accept Medicaid/Apple Health, so we're able to support a wide range of the families you serve.

I'd love to connect and share more about what we offer, and explore how we might refer families to each other's care. Would you have 15–20 minutes for a call, or would you be open to us leaving some information with your practice?

With gratitude for the work you do,
Darlene Johnson
Founder, Womb & Root Birthing Services
(206) 409-9747 · info@wombandrootbirth.com · wombandrootbirth.com
