# Build the November Newsletter in Kit

*Step-by-step recipe. The words live in `newsletters/2026-11-newsletter.md`. The easiest way is to
copy each box from `newsletters/2026-11-kit-paste.html`, in order.*

---

## Step 1: Start a broadcast

1. Go to **Send**, then **Broadcasts**, then **New broadcast**.
2. **Tip:** if Kit lets you **duplicate** October's broadcast, do that. Your template, colors, and
   sign-off will already be in place. Then replace each section.

## Step 2: Subject line and preview text

- **Subject:** Gathering in, with gratitude 🕯️
- **Preview text:** A gratitude ritual for your body, the art of receiving, and holiday love for new mamas.

## Step 3: Add the content, top to bottom

1. **Header image:** candlelight, autumn branches, or a warm table (optional)
2. **A letter from Darlene**
3. **Feminine energy practice: Hands of Thanks**, with its steps and journal prompt
4. **Wellness over 40: The art of receiving**
5. **Pregnancy & postpartum corner**: holidays with a newborn, the worksheet gift, military
   families, and the PSI helpline and 988
6. **From our circle**: full-time news, open spots, and payment options
7. **Let's begin with a conversation**, then add a **Button**:
   - Text: **Explore postpartum care**
   - Link: **https://www.wombandrootbirth.com**
   - Color: deep sage `#879679` or logo green `#6F7D62`
   - Discovery calls are scheduled **by reply**, so there's no contact page link this month.
8. **A blessing to carry**, with your sign-off

## Step 4: Recipients

Send to **all subscribers**. If you've added new clients (tagged "Clients"), they'll receive it too.

## Step 5: Preview and test

1. Use **Preview** to check the desktop and mobile views.
2. **Send a test email** to yourself. Open it on your phone and tap the button.
3. Read it once aloud. If anything doesn't sound like you, change it.

## Step 6: Schedule

- **Suggested:** Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at 7:30 AM Pacific (October's issue also went out on
  a Wednesday morning).
- Any send before **mid-November** keeps the holiday tips timely (Thanksgiving is November 26).

## After it's sent

- **Watch for "worksheet" replies,** and send the Postpartum Planning Worksheet to each person who
  asks. Each reply is a warm lead. Consider following up a few days later with a gentle
  discovery call invitation.
- About a week later, look at **opens and clicks** in Kit, and share them with Claude.
