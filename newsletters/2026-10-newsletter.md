## 🌿 Feminine energy practice: *Rooting Breath* (5 minutes)

When life pulls you in many directions, return home to your body.

1. Sit or stand with your feet flat on the floor. Feel the ground beneath you.
2. Place one hand low on your belly, over your womb space, and one hand on your heart.
3. Breathe in slowly through your nose, imagining roots growing down from your feet into the earth.
4. Breathe out through soft lips, letting your shoulders melt down.
5. Repeat for 10 breaths. On the last one, whisper: *"I am rooted. I am held. I am enough."*

**Journal prompt:** *What am I ready to let fall away this season, like the autumn leaves?*

## 🍂 Wellness over 40: *Your wisdom season*

October 18 is World Menopause Day, a beautiful reminder that the years of perimenopause and
beyond are not an ending. They are an initiation.

Many women over 40 find their bodies asking for something new: more rest, more nourishment,
more honesty about what they will and won't carry. This isn't your body failing you. It is your
body inviting you to listen more deeply.

**This month, try one gentle act of listening:**
- Go to bed 20 minutes earlier, three nights this week.
- Swap one rushed meal for a warm, slow one, such as a nourishing soup or stew.
- Ask yourself each morning: *"What do I need today?"*, and honor the answer.

You are not behind. You are ripening. 🌾

*If you're experiencing changes that concern you, please talk with your healthcare provider.
You deserve care and answers.*

## 🤱 Pregnancy & postpartum corner

**Warmth for the fourth trimester.** Many traditions around the world keep new mothers warm after
birth, with cozy socks, warm drinks, and soups and stews instead of cold foods. Autumn is a
beautiful time to prepare: if you're expecting this winter, consider asking loved ones to fill
your freezer with warm, nourishing meals now.

Our postpartum doulas can help with meal preparation, feeding support, and overnight care, so you
can rest and heal. 💛

**A tender note:** October is Pregnancy and Infant Loss Awareness Month. If you are carrying the
ache of a loss, we see you and we honor your baby. On October 15 at 7pm, families around the
world light a candle in remembrance. You are welcome to join us in lighting one.
