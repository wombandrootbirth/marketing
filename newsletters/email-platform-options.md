# Email Platform Options — Research (October 2026)

*Darlene wants a free way to send her monthly newsletter instead of paying for Flodesk.
List size: about 3,180 subscribers. Prices and limits change, so check before deciding.*

| Option | Cost for a list this size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Kit (free Newsletter plan)** | **Free** up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited emails | Best free fit. Small Kit branding and one Kit-managed "recommendation" slot. Free forms and simple templates. [Kit help](https://help.kit.com/en/articles/16627071-the-kit-free-plan) |
| Shopify Email | 10,000 free emails/month | Needs an **active paid Shopify plan**. Likely unavailable while the store is closed (confirm with Shopify). Sends as Her Secret Garden, a different brand. [Pricing](https://www.sequenzy.com/pricing/shopify-email) |
| Squarespace Email Campaigns | Free trial of 3 campaigns, then from $8/month for 500 sends (about $20/month for this list) | Separate add-on. The trial has Squarespace branding and no test emails. [Review](https://www.stylefactoryproductions.com/blog/squarespace-email-campaigns-review) |
| Sender | Free up to 2,500 subscribers | Too small, unless the list is cleaned to engaged readers |
| MailerLite / Mailchimp | Free up to 250 subscribers | Too small |
| Brevo | Free, but 300 emails/day | Would take about 11 days to reach the whole list |

Sources: [EmailTooltester free newsletter platforms](https://www.emailtooltester.com/en/blog/free-newsletter-platforms/)

## Decision (Oct 4, 2026)

Darlene chose **Kit's free plan** and imported 2,311 subscribers (everyone who had ever opened).

## Recommendation

1. Use **Kit's free plan** for the Womb & Root newsletter.
2. **Export subscribers from Flodesk** before canceling.
3. Consider importing only **engaged** readers. Many contacts came from old Wix, Shopify, and Vagaro
   imports, and sending only to people who open helps emails land in inboxes.
4. If the Shopify store reopens, use **Shopify Email** for Her Secret Garden customers.
5. For one-on-one client conversations, use regular email (info@wombandrootbirth.com).

## Timing

- If Flodesk is paid through October, send October from Flodesk and switch to Kit for November.
- If not, set up Kit first; October goes out a few days later.
