## 🌿 Feminine energy practice: *Hands of Thanks* (5 minutes)

Before giving thanks for everything around you, give thanks to the body that carries you.

1. Sit somewhere quiet, and rub your palms together until they feel warm.
2. Place your warm hands over your heart. Breathe in slowly, and whisper: *"Thank you for loving."*
3. Move your hands to your belly, over your womb space. Breathe, and whisper: *"Thank you for creating."*
4. Rest your hands on your thighs. Breathe, and whisper: *"Thank you for carrying me."*
5. Finally, hold your own hands. Breathe, and whisper: *"Thank you for all you give."*

**Journal prompt:** *What has my body done for me this year that I haven't yet thanked her for?*

## 🍂 Wellness over 40: *The art of receiving*

So many of us are wonderful at giving. We cook the meal, remember the birthdays, and hold
everyone's feelings. But receiving? That can feel harder than it should.

In the years after 40, many women find that their bodies and spirits are asking for more care,
not less. Receiving is not weakness. It is part of the natural rhythm of giving, like breathing
out and breathing in.

**This month, practice receiving in small ways:**
- When someone offers help, say *"Yes, thank you,"* before you can talk yourself out of it.
- Let a compliment land. Simply say *"Thank you,"* with no explaining it away.
- Choose one holiday task to share or set down completely.

You have poured into so many cups. This season, let yours be filled, too. 🌾

*If you're experiencing changes in your body or mood that concern you, please talk with your
healthcare provider. You deserve care and answers.*

## 🤱 Pregnancy & postpartum corner

**The holidays with a newborn.** If you're expecting or newly postpartum this season, it's okay
to keep your circle small. You might:

- Let loved ones visit for short stays, and ask each guest to bring a dish or do one chore
- Stay home instead of traveling, and invite family to come to you, if and when you're ready
- Choose one person to share your boundaries kindly for you
