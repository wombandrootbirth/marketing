# Build the October Newsletter in Kit

*Step-by-step recipe. The words live in `newsletters/2026-10-newsletter.md`. Kit's screens change
from time to time, so if a button has a slightly different name, look for the closest match.*

---

## Your Kit list

- **2,311 subscribers**, imported from Flodesk on Oct 4, 2026: everyone who had ever opened an email.
  They carry the tag **"Imported from Flodesk."**
- **Free plan** (up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited emails).

## Step 1: Check your sender email (one time only)

1. In Kit, open **Settings**, then **Email**.
2. Make sure your **sending address** is **info@wombandrootbirth.com** with the name
   **Darlene | Womb & Root**, and that it's **verified** (Kit emails you a confirmation link).
3. If Kit offers to **authenticate your domain** (wombandrootbirth.com), follow its steps. It helps
   your emails land in inboxes instead of spam, which matters most with Gmail and Yahoo. The steps
   happen in Squarespace's domain settings. Claude can walk you through it.

## Step 2: Start a broadcast

1. Go to **Send**, then **Broadcasts**, then **New broadcast**.
2. Choose a simple, clean template. Pick one with a light background and room for one image at the top.

## Step 3: Subject line and preview text

- **Subject:** Let yourself be rooted this October 🍂
- **Preview text:** A grounding practice, wisdom for women over 40, and a little love for new mamas.

## Step 4: Add the content, top to bottom

Copy each section from `newsletters/2026-10-newsletter.md` into the email:

1. **Header image:** autumn leaves or tree roots (optional, but lovely)
2. **A letter from Darlene**
3. **Feminine energy practice: Rooting Breath**, with its steps and journal prompt
4. **Wellness over 40: Your wisdom season**
5. **Pregnancy & postpartum corner**, including the tender note, the PSI helpline, and 988
6. **From our circle**: the belly binding spotlight and News from Womb & Root
7. **Let's begin with a conversation**, then add a **Button**:
   - Text: **Book your free discovery call**
   - Link: **https://www.wombandrootbirth.com/contact**
   - Color: deep sage `#879679` or logo green `#6F7D62`
8. **A blessing to carry**, with your sign-off

Tips:
- Keep paragraphs short, so they read well on phones.
- Use **one** button only. Your readers rarely click, so one clear invitation works best.
- To link words, highlight them and use the link icon.

## Step 5: Recipients

Send to **all subscribers**. (Right now, that's everyone tagged "Imported from Flodesk.")

## Step 6: Preview and test

1. Use **Preview** to check desktop and mobile views.
2. **Send a test email** to yourself. Open it on your phone and tap the button.
3. Read it once aloud. If anything doesn't sound like you, change it.

## Step 7: Schedule

- **Suggested:** Wednesday, October 7, 2026, at 7:30 AM Pacific.
- Any send before **October 15** keeps the candle-lighting invitation timely.

## After it's sent

- About a week later, look at **opens and clicks** in Kit's broadcast report, and share them with
  Claude for ideas for November.
- Once this first Kit newsletter has gone out smoothly, you can **cancel Flodesk**.
