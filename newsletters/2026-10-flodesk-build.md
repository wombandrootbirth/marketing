# Build the October Newsletter in Flodesk

*Step-by-step recipe. The words live in `newsletters/2026-10-newsletter.md`. This guide tells you
where each piece goes in Flodesk.*

---

## What we learned from your Flodesk account

- **Your list:** about 3,180 subscribers.
- **Your saved design:** a template called **"Newsletter Template,"** used for your 2025
  newsletters. We'll reuse it, so October looks like you.
- **Opens:** your newsletters were usually opened by 14–20% of readers, and your
  January 1 "Goddess Power" issue by 33%. That's healthy for a list your size.
- **Clicks:** very few readers clicked a link (under 1%). So this issue has **one clear
  button**, placed where readers are most warmed up.
- **Best days:** Monday and Wednesday perform about the same (around 20–21% opens).
- **Last newsletter to everyone:** March 2025. After a long pause, some readers may have
  forgotten you, so the letter gently reintroduces you.

---

## Step 1: Start from your template

1. In Flodesk, go to **Emails**.
2. Find **"Spring Into Renewal: Your Goddess Detox Guide"** (your March 2025 newsletter, built on
   your Newsletter Template), click the **⋯** menu, and choose **Duplicate**.
3. Rename the copy **"October 2026 — The Season of Rooting."**

This keeps your colors, fonts, logo, and footer exactly as they were.

## Step 2: Fill in the blocks, top to bottom

Delete any old blocks you don't need, and add new ones with the **+** button.
"Paste" means copy that section from `newsletters/2026-10-newsletter.md`.

| # | Flodesk block | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Image** (header) | Your logo or an autumn image (photo idea 1). Title text on the image: *The Season of Rooting* |
| 2 | **Image + Text layout** | Your photo (photo idea 2) + paste **"A letter from Darlene."** |
| 3 | **Divider** | A thin line to rest the eye |
| 4 | **Text** | Paste **"Feminine energy practice: Rooting Breath"** with its steps and journal prompt |
| 5 | **Image** | Photo idea 3 (hands resting on belly or heart), optional |
| 6 | **Text** | Paste **"Wellness over 40: Your wisdom season"** |
| 7 | **Divider** | |
| 8 | **Text** | Paste **"Pregnancy & postpartum corner,"** including the tender note and helpline |
| 9 | **Image + Text layout** | Belly binding photo (photo idea 6) + paste **"From our circle"** and your agency news |
| 10 | **Text** | Paste **"Let's begin with a conversation"** (without the bracketed link text) |
| 11 | **Button** | Text: **Book your free discovery call** · Link: your scheduler link |
| 12 | **Text** (centered, italic) | Paste **"A blessing to carry"** and your sign-off |
| 13 | **Footer** | Keep your template's footer. Check your address and social links |

**Tips:**
- Make the button deep sage (`#879679`) or logo green (`#6F7D62`), so it's the one strong
  color the eye lands on.
- Keep text blocks short on phones. If a section feels long, split it into two text blocks
  with a little space between.
- In Flodesk, highlight text and click the **link icon** to link words.

## Step 3: Subject line and preview text

- **Subject:** Let yourself be rooted this October 🍂
- **Preview text:** A grounding practice, wisdom for women over 40, and a little love for new mamas.

(Alternate subjects are in the newsletter file if you'd like a different feeling.)

## Step 4: Who receives it

- **Send to:** All subscribers.
- **Optional:** your November 2025 yoni steam email left out the **"Birth and Postpartum
  Clients"** group. For a newsletter, it's lovely to *include* them. It keeps past clients
  close and makes referrals more likely.

## Step 5: Preview and test

1. Click **Preview**, and check both the **desktop** and **mobile** views.
2. Send a **test email** to yourself. Open it on your phone and tap the button to make sure
   the link works.
3. Read it once aloud. If anything doesn't sound like you, change it.

## Step 6: Schedule it

- **Suggested:** **Wednesday, October 7, 2026, at 7:30 AM** (Pacific), matching your usual
  Wednesday-morning rhythm. If you'd rather send sooner, Wednesday, September 30 or
  Thursday, October 1 works too.
- The Pregnancy & Infant Loss Remembrance candle lighting is **October 15**, so any send
  before then keeps that invitation timely.

## After it's sent

opens and clicks in Flodesk and suggest what to try next month.
