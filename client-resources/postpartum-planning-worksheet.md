**A note to you, mama:**
The weeks after birth are a sacred time of rest and healing. In many cultures, mothers are cared
for by their village for 40 days. This worksheet helps you gather your village, so you can be held
while you heal. There are no wrong answers. Fill in what you know, and we'll explore the rest together. 🌿

## 🚨 Warning signs after birth

**Call 911** if you have:
- Chest pain, or trouble breathing
- A seizure
- Thoughts of hurting yourself or your baby

**Call your provider right away** if you have:
- Bleeding that soaks through a pad in an hour or less, or large clots
- A fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher
- A headache that won't go away, or changes in your vision
- A cesarean incision that's red, swollen, leaking, or opening
- Pain, swelling, or redness in one leg
- Feeling like something is very wrong

*Trust yourself, mama. If something feels wrong, call. You will never be a bother.*
