# Client Resources

*Forms and handouts Darlene shares with Womb & Root clients. **All client forms are electronic and
live in Doulado** (no Canva or printing needed). Copy each one into a Doulado form.*

## Birth plans (`birth-plans/`)

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `hospital-birth-plan.md` | Most clients. Includes a cesarean section. |
| `birth-center-birth-plan.md` | Birth center births. Includes going home and a transfer plan. |
| `home-birth-plan.md` | Home births. Includes preparing the home, supplies, and a transfer plan. |

**How to use:** send it through Doulado, then review it together at a prenatal visit (around 32–36
weeks). Ask the client to share it with her provider and keep a copy on her phone. Keep it to 1–2 pages if you can;
care teams read short plans more closely.
