# Client Resources

*Handouts and forms Darlene shares with Womb & Root clients. Copy any of these into Google Docs or
Canva, add the brand colors and logo, and print or share as a PDF.*

## Birth plans (`birth-plans/`)

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `hospital-birth-plan.md` | Most clients. Includes a cesarean section. |
| `birth-center-birth-plan.md` | Birth center births. Includes going home and a transfer plan. |
| `home-birth-plan.md` | Home births. Includes preparing the home, supplies, and a transfer plan. |

**How to use:** fill one out together at a prenatal visit (around 32–36 weeks). Ask the client to
review it with her provider, then keep printed copies in her bag. Keep it to 1–2 pages if you can;
care teams read short plans more closely.
