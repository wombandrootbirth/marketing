## 🌿 During labor

**The room**
- ☐ Dim lights and a quiet, calm space
- ☐ My own music, a diffuser (if allowed), or my own pillow and gown
- ☐ Please keep the number of people in the room small
- ☐ I prefer [ female staff / no students or residents / no preference ]

**Moving and comfort**
- ☐ Freedom to walk, sway, and change positions
- ☐ Birth ball, peanut ball, shower, or tub, if available
- ☐ Eat light snacks and drink clear fluids, as allowed
- ☐ Intermittent monitoring if baby and I are doing well, or wireless monitoring if continuous is needed
- ☐ A saline lock instead of a continuous IV, unless one is needed

**Pain relief** (I would like to…)
- ☐ Labor without medication. Please don't offer it; I'll ask if I want it.
- ☐ Try comfort measures first (breathing, massage, counter-pressure, water, rebozo), then decide
- ☐ Have an epidural when I ask for it
- ☐ Consider nitrous oxide or IV pain medication
- ☐ Undecided. I'd like to keep my options open.

**Interventions**
- ☐ Please talk with me before any intervention (breaking my water, Pitocin, induction), and give me time to decide when it's safe
- ☐ Let labor begin on its own when possible

## 🌸 Pushing and birth

- ☐ Push when I feel the urge (rather than counted, directed pushing), unless needed
- ☐ Choose my own position: hands and knees, side-lying, squatting, or a squat bar
- ☐ Warm compresses and gentle support to help protect my perineum
- ☐ A mirror, so I can see baby crowning
- ☐ I would like to touch baby's head as baby crowns
- ☐ My partner would like to announce the sex / catch baby / cut the cord

## 💛 After baby arrives

- ☐ Immediate skin-to-skin, with baby placed on my chest
- ☐ Delayed cord clamping (wait until the cord stops pulsing, or at least 1–3 minutes)
- ☐ Cord blood banking: ☐ yes ☐ no
- ☐ An uninterrupted **golden hour** before weighing, measuring, and newborn checks
- ☐ Placenta: ☐ hospital may dispose of it ☐ **we're keeping it for encapsulation** (please bag it and refrigerate it within 1 hour)

## 🤍 If a cesarean birth becomes necessary

- ☐ My partner and my doula stay with me (doula as hospital policy allows)
- ☐ A clear drape, or the drape lowered, so I can see baby born
- ☐ Skin-to-skin in the operating room, if baby and I are well

- ☐ Please explain what's happening as you go
- ☐ My own music in the operating room
- ☐ Delayed cord clamping, if safe
- ☐ Baby stays with me in recovery, and we begin breastfeeding as soon as possible
