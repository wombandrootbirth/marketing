## 🌿 During labor

**Arriving and the room**
- ☐ I plan to labor at home until [ my midwife advises / contractions are ___ minutes apart ]
- ☐ Dim lights, quiet voices, and calm
- ☐ My own music, essential oils, pillow, and comfort items
- ☐ I'd like these people present: _______________________
- ☐ Children present? ☐ yes, with a dedicated caregiver: _______ ☐ no

**Comfort**
- ☐ Freedom to walk, sway, and change positions
- ☐ Labor tub or shower
- ☐ Birth ball, peanut ball, rebozo, or birth stool
- ☐ Massage, counter-pressure, hot or cold compresses
- ☐ Eat and drink freely
- ☐ Nitrous oxide, if offered, and only if I ask
- ☐ Quiet encouragement, or ☐ please talk me through each contraction

## 🌸 Pushing and birth

- ☐ **Water birth**, if possible
- ☐ Land birth, in the position that feels right to me
- ☐ Push when I feel the urge
- ☐ Warm compresses and perineal support
- ☐ I'd like to reach down and touch baby's head
- ☐ I would like to catch baby · ☐ my partner would like to catch baby (with my midwife's guidance)
- ☐ My partner would like to announce the sex / cut the cord

## 💛 After baby arrives

- ☐ Immediate skin-to-skin
- ☐ Delayed cord clamping (wait until the cord stops pulsing)
- ☐ An uninterrupted **golden hour**, with newborn exams done on my chest if possible
- ☐ Placenta: ☐ the birth center may dispose of it ☐ **we're keeping it for encapsulation** (please bag it and refrigerate it within 1 hour)
- ☐ I'd like to see the placenta and have it explained to me
