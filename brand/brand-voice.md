# Womb and Root Birthing Agency — Brand Voice

## Who we are

A holistic birthing agency offering feminine energy embodiment coaching, postpartum doula care,
and womb wellness support, with a team of doulas serving families through pregnancy, birth,
and the sacred postpartum season.

- Founder: Darlene Johnson
- Areas served: South King County and Pierce County, Washington
- Website: www.wombandrootbirth.com
- How to connect: book a free discovery call, or reach us by email

## Who we serve

Mothers-to-be and new mothers, pregnant or postpartum, who:

- are drawn to a more holistic, body-honoring birth and postpartum experience
- want a trusted birth worker beside them through pregnancy, labor and delivery, and postpartum
- need real, hands-on postpartum help: day or overnight support, lactation support,
  meal preparation, rest, and healing

**What she is feeling:** she longs to be supported. She doesn't want to carry this season alone.
She wants someone knowledgeable and nurturing to hold space for her body, her baby, and her heart.

## How we sound

- **Feminine** — soft, warm, nurturing
- **Patient** — never rushed, never pressuring
- **Spiritual** — honoring the body, the womb, and the sacred seasons of life

## Words we love

Hold space · nurture · sacred · rooted · honor · embodied · gentle · wisdom · return home to yourself

## Words we avoid

Hustle · fix · urgent / "act now" · cold clinical language

## Our offerings

1. **Feminine energy embodiment coaching:** tuning fork and Reiki meditation, plus 1-on-1 coaching
   and digital offerings that help a woman return to confidence in her body and her abilities.
2. **Postpartum doula care:** support at home, or in the hospital after a cesarean birth.
   Lactation support, holistic postpartum healing, and emotional support, day or overnight.
3. **Womb wellness:** yoni steams, belly binding, and placenta encapsulation.
