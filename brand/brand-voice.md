## Who we serve

Mothers-to-be and new mothers, pregnant or postpartum, who:

- are drawn to a more holistic, body-honoring birth and postpartum experience
- want a trusted birth worker beside them through pregnancy, labor and delivery, and postpartum
- need real, hands-on postpartum help: day or overnight support, lactation support,
  meal preparation, rest, and healing

**What she is feeling:** she longs to be supported. She doesn't want to carry this season alone.
She wants someone knowledgeable and nurturing to hold space for her body, her baby, and her heart.
