## Core Packages

Every package is built around two foundations, **Day Support** and **Overnight Support**, with
holistic add-ons woven in. Day Support runs 4–6 hours and flexes to your household's needs.
Overnight Support is a steady 8-hour shift, 10pm–6am, so you can actually sleep.

### Moonlit Nest · Overnight Support
- 8 hours of overnight care, 10pm–6am
- Newborn overnight care & feeding support (bottle or pumped milk, or gentle wake-support for
  breastfeeding)
- Light nursery reset before your Practitioner leaves
- Morning recap so you wake up informed, not worried
- **$600 / night** · 3+ nights per week: **$550 / night**

### Full Circle Care · Day Support + Overnight Support
- One Day Support visit (4–6 hrs) paired with one Overnight Support shift (8 hrs) in the same
  24-hour cycle
- Continuous, full-circle care: someone in your corner day and night
- Includes everything in Moonlit Nest, plus daytime newborn care, light meal/household support,
  and emotional check-ins
- **Starting at $810** (an 8% savings vs. booking Day + Overnight separately; final total depends
  on Day Support hours selected)

### Steam & Settle · Day Support + Postpartum Yoni Steam
- One Day Support visit (4–6 hrs) of newborn care, recovery support & light household help
- One postpartum yoni steam session (45 min) for physical & energetic restoration
- Steam session bundled at $125 (a $25 savings off the standalone $150 rate)
- **Starting at $405** (final total depends on Day Support hours selected)

### Nourished Days · Day Support + Postpartum Meal Prep
- One Day Support visit (4–6 hrs) of newborn care, recovery support & light household help
- 4 hours of postpartum meal prep: nourishing, freezer-ready meals for the days ahead
- Meal prep bundled at $245 (a $30 savings off the standalone $275 rate)
- **Starting at $525** (final total depends on Day Support hours selected)
