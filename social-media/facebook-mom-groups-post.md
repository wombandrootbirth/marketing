## Tips for posting

- **Read each group's rules first.** Many mom groups only allow business posts on certain days
  (for example "Promo Friday"), or ask you to check with the admin. A kind note to the admin
  builds trust: *"Hi! I'm a local postpartum doula. May I share a short post about our services?"*
- **Add a photo.** A warm photo of you gets far more attention than text alone.
- **Space it out.** Post in 2–3 groups a day, not all at once. Facebook may treat the same post
  in many groups on the same day as spam.
- **Reply privately.** When someone comments, answer kindly in the comments, then send a message.
  Insurance details are private, so keep those conversations in messages.
- **Keep a list.** Note which groups you posted in and when, so you can return in about a month
  with a fresh post.
