# Content Calendar — 6 Week Postpartum Healing Journey

*Built from Darlene's guide, "6 Week Postpartum Healing Journey" (Canva design, 54 pages; PDF in
Google Drive under "6 Week Postpartum Healing Program"). Six weeks of content: one week of the
guide per week of posts, Oct 12 – Nov 22, 2026.*

**Rhythm (fits a 9-to-5):** 3 feed posts a week (Mon · Wed · Fri) for Instagram and Facebook, plus
2–3 quick stories. Batch-create on the weekend.

**Every post ends with one gentle invitation.** Rotate between:
- "The full 6 Week Postpartum Healing Journey is waiting for you. [link to guide]"
- "Want support in person? Book a free discovery call: wombandrootbirth.com/contact"

**Hashtags (pick 8–10):** #postpartumdoula #fourthtrimester #postpartumhealing #holisticpostpartum
#tacomadoula #piercecountymoms #southkingcounty #newmomsupport #womenshealing #wombwellness

> **Herbs and health notes:** every herb post includes *"Please check with your provider before
> using herbs, especially while breastfeeding."* Never promise medical results. Speak of practices
> as traditional and supportive.

## Before launching

- [ ] **Where can people get the guide, and what does it cost?** Add the link to every
      "[link to guide]." (Website shop? Kit's free product feature? Shopify, once reopened?)
- [ ] **Comfrey:** the guide lists comfrey as a **tea** in Week 5. Comfrey is generally advised
      for **external use only** (sitz baths, steams, and compresses), and not for drinking,
      especially while breastfeeding. Consider updating the Week 5 tea list.
- [ ] **Name on the guide:** the cover says "Darlene Charise," and the copyright says
      "Darlene JOhnson" (typo). Choose one name, to match Womb & Root.
- [ ] Create graphics in Canva using your brand colors (cream, sage, logo green) and fonts.
