## Tips

- **Add a photo of you.** On a personal page, a warm, smiling photo of you (or your hands
  holding a binding cloth or a cup of tea) gets far more love than a graphic.
- **Reply to every comment** and thank everyone who shares. It helps the post reach more people.
- **Pin it** to the top of your profile for October.
- **Post it again in mid-November** with updated openings for December.
