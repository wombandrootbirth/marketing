# 🌿 Start Here — How to Use Your Marketing Helpers

*A gentle guide for Darlene. Come back to it whenever you need a reminder.*

Think of this project as a **filing cabinet**, and your helpers (agents) as **three assistants**
who read from it and write into it. The more you keep in the cabinet, the better they help you.

---

## Your filing cabinet

| Drawer (folder) | What goes in it | Who fills it |
|---|---|---|
| `brand/` | Who you are, your voice, offerings, and look | You, rarely updated |
| `organizer/agency-notebook.md` | **The heart of everything:** team, open spots, insurance, tasks, ideas, partners | You and the Agency Assistant, weekly |
| `organizer/weekly-plans/` | One plan per week | Agency Assistant |
| `marketing-plan/weekly-lists/` | One outreach list per week | Client Finder |
| `marketing-plan/discovery-call-invitation.md` | Ready words to invite people to a discovery call | Ready to use |
| `newsletters/` | One newsletter and one Flodesk build guide per month (plus a Canva guide for graphics) | Newsletter Writer |
| `social-media/` | Posts for Facebook, Instagram, and more | You and Claude |

**The golden rule:** your helpers only know what's written in the cabinet. When something changes
(a new doula, open spots, a partner replies, TRICARE is approved), tell a helper and it will write
it in the notebook.

**Keep these out of the cabinet:**
- **Private client information.** Names, health details, and birth stories belong in your secure
  client system, not here.
- **Photos.** Keep them in Google Drive or Canva, where they're easy to use.

---

## Your three helpers

| Helper | What to say | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency Assistant** | "Plan my week." | Top 3 priorities, what's coming up, your next offering idea, a social media rhythm, admin reminders, and rest |
| **Client Finder** | "Make my weekly marketing list." | People and places to reach out to, with messages ready to send, plus posts and a past-client check-in |
| **Newsletter Writer** | "Write next month's newsletter." | A full newsletter, plus a step-by-step Flodesk build guide |

**How to start:** go to **claude.ai/code**, choose your **marketing** project, and type your
request in plain words.

---

## Your rhythm

### 🌙 Every Monday (about 20 minutes)

1. **Update your notebook first.** For example: *"Update my notebook: I have 2 postpartum
   overnight spots open in November, and my doula [name] is available weekends."*
2. Say **"Plan my week."**
3. Say **"Make my weekly marketing list."**
4. Copy what you'll use into Google Docs, and go live your week.

### 🌸 Around the 20th of each month

Say **"Write next month's newsletter."** Review it, add your news, then build it in Flodesk
using that month's build guide (for example `newsletters/2026-10-flodesk-build.md`).

### ✨ Anytime

- *"Remember this idea: a Wise Woman circle in January."* It's saved to your notebook.
- *"Write 3 Instagram posts about belly binding."*
- *"A midwife replied. Help me write back."*

---

## Getting the best from your helpers

- **Give feedback freely.** "Make it softer," "shorter," or "that doesn't sound like me" all help.
- **Ask for changes to stick.** If you ask for the same thing twice, say: *"Update the Newsletter
  Writer so it always does this."* Then it becomes permanent.
- **Check facts before posting,** such as insurance details, dates, and prices. Helpers mark
  anything uncertain with **[brackets]** or **"(look up & verify)."**
- **Start small.** Use one helper at a time until it feels natural. The Monday rhythm alone will
  carry you far.

---

## Pasting into Google Docs

To keep headings and bold text when you paste, first turn on
**Tools → Preferences → Enable Markdown** in Google Docs. Then use **Edit → Paste from Markdown**.

## A few words you'll hear

- **File:** one document, like a page in a binder.
- **Folder:** a group of files, like a drawer.
- **Agent:** a helper with one job and its own written instructions.
- **Commit:** saving a snapshot of your work with a short note.
- **Push:** sending saved work up to GitHub, where it's kept safe online.
- **Branch:** a draft copy of your project where new work happens.
- **Pull request / merge:** reviewing a draft copy and adding it to the main, official copy.

*You're building something beautiful, one gentle step at a time.* 🌸
