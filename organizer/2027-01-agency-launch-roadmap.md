# Agency Launch Roadmap · January 2027

*A gentle, week-by-week path to opening Womb & Root as a postpartum agency with a team. Darlene
keeps serving her own clients the whole time, so each week holds only 3–5 launch tasks.*

**Launch goal:** welcome the first Womb & Root team doula(s) and announce the agency in **January 2027**.

> **Items marked "confirm"** are rules that depend on Washington law or Darlene's setup. Check them
> with a free advisor at the **Washington SBDC** (Small Business Development Center), an
> accountant, or an attorney before acting on them.

---

## Phase 1 · Foundation (Oct 26 – Nov 15)

**Week of Oct 26** (recovering from COVID, so keep it light)
- [ ] Add images to the November newsletter, send a test, and schedule it for **Wed, Nov 4, 5 AM**
- [ ] Finalize the **doula employment ad** (draft only; don't post yet)
- [ ] Book a free **Washington SBDC** advising appointment: employees vs. contractors, payroll, and insurance (confirm)
- [ ] Submit the **Swedish doula program invoice** by midnight Nov 1

**Week of Nov 2**
- [ ] SBDC or accountant meeting: decide **employees or independent contractors**. Washington is strict about who counts as a contractor, and employees need workers' comp through L&I (confirm)
- [ ] Get **liability insurance** quotes that cover a team (professional + general liability)
- [ ] List what every Womb & Root doula needs: training, CPR/First Aid, background check, food handler's card (for meal prep), vaccination policy, and Washington doula certification for Apple Health billing (confirm)

**Week of Nov 9**
- [ ] Set **team pay rates**, using your services menu prices so the agency stays healthy
- [ ] Draft the **doula agreement** (contractor or employee) and a **confidentiality agreement**
- [ ] Update your **client contract** so it covers care by a Womb & Root team doula

## Phase 2 · Building the team (Nov 16 – Dec 13)

**Week of Nov 16**
- [ ] **Post the employment ad** (doula training programs, local doula groups, Facebook groups, DoulaMatch, Indeed)
- [ ] Finalize **onboarding paperwork**: application, agreement, W-9 or W-4, certifications checklist
- [ ] Write **interview questions** that reflect your values: rest, village care, holistic healing

**Weeks of Nov 23 and Nov 30** (Thanksgiving week: keep it soft)
- [ ] Review applications and hold **interviews**
- [ ] Write a short **Womb & Root Standards of Care** handbook: how we show up, communicate, and care for mothers

**Week of Dec 7**
- [ ] Make offers to your first doula(s); start background checks and paperwork
- [ ] Set up **Doulado** for the team: scheduling, client notes, and forms (check whether your plan includes team members)

## Phase 3 · Getting ready to open (Dec 14 – Jan 3)

- [ ] **Orientation and training** with your new doula(s): shadow visits with your own clients
- [ ] Set up **payroll** (or contractor payments) and a simple way to track hours
- [ ] Update the **website**: "Meet our doulas" and team bios (Darlene does this; Claude can write the bios)
- [ ] Update the **brochure** in Canva to say "our doulas," and plan a reorder
- [ ] Draft the **January launch announcement** for the newsletter and social media

## Phase 4 · Launch (January 2027)

- [ ] 🎉 **Send the launch newsletter** and post the announcement
- [ ] Email referral partners with the news (midwives, IBCLCs, pelvic floor therapists, Open Arms, Gwen)
- [ ] Update open spots: how many families can the team now welcome each month?
- [ ] Celebrate. You built a village. 🌿
