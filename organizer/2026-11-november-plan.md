### Holistic and herbal postpartum care to share

- **Sitz baths and peri care:** calendula, lavender, yarrow, and witch hazel. Comfrey is for outside use only, never as a tea.
- **Nourishing teas:** nettle, oatstraw, red raspberry leaf, chamomile, and lemon balm. Fennel and oats are traditional for milk supply. *(Peppermint and sage may lower milk supply for some mothers.)*
- **Warming foods:** bone broth, ginger, cinnamon, oats, dates, soups, and stews, part of many cultures' first-40-days traditions
- **Belly binding:** gentle support for the belly and hips as the body recovers
- **Postpartum yoni steam:** once cleared by a provider (Steam & Settle)
- **Placenta encapsulation**
- **Tuning fork and Reiki meditation:** rest for the nervous system
- **Rest and the first 40 days:** staying close to bed, warmth, and receiving care

**Always say:** *"Please check with your provider before using herbs, especially while breastfeeding."* Speak of practices as traditional and supportive, and never promise medical results.
